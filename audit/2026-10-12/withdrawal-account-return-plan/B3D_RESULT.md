# B3d Records UI — accepted at the bounded QA scope

The Records UI now exposes the B3b/B3c owner-funds remedies. Owner/Admin can correct a current withdrawal's account or amount, record actual money received against it, and correct or cancel that receipt. The detail shows linked receipt history and current totals. Manager sees the facts without the write action. The forms provide account/date selection, before/after review, excess acknowledgement, retained drafts on failure, current-facts refresh, and a persistent saved state.

The final-source isolated QA app and test APKs built successfully with `:app:assembleDebug :app:assembleDebugAndroidTest -Psaaf.instrumentationSandbox=true`.
