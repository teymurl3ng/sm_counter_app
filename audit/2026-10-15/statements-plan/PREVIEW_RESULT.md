16 October 2026. Baseline `646c219`. ✅ ~~Statement snapshot and preview, including the bounded customer-detail reorganisation.~~ PDF/image export and manual message preparation remain the next two existing pieces; phase5 as a whole stays open.

Customer detail now presents one balance/status/terms summary, one Statements & reminders entry and purposeful history/profile/credit disclosures. Pending/held attention stays visible, with Review opening and bringing the relevant section into view. Profile editing and all existing Owner credit confirmations remain available, with their authorization unchanged. The same customer's disclosure and scroll state survive a visit to the statement pane. Nested management content reuses the enclosing section rather than adding cards inside cards; progress has a reserved slot in the summary.

Statement preview reads shop/customer facts, period opening/closing totals, original movements, correction deltas and catalog product/unit detail in one read transaction. Three parameterized DAO queries support those reads without changing existing queries, ledger logic, schemas, sync or permissions. Cumulative amounts use the existing canonical SQL rounding; displayed movements are differences between successive rounded running balances, with a visible explanation when required. A final reconciliation check refuses inconsistent snapshots; today's balance also has to match the canonical full account balance. Past-period statements remain separate from current debt. Private customer/staff notes and Owner flags are excluded from the statement facts.

The existing Customers host owns one header/footer while its body crossfades between detail and statement. Period choices are Last7days, This month and a native custom date range. Old work is cancelled on period changes; failed requests preserve a faded, explicitly previous preview with retry/revert. The controller checks session, selected customer and destination before and after loading. Native text on light document paper is directly accessible and scalable in either app theme; no bitmap is required to read this first preview. Full source references are retained. Page selection clamps after refresh if activity shrinks.
