### ✅ ~~L1 — Make credit amounts and warnings useful~~

Implemented and accepted with affected isolated QA; see [phase 3 result](audit/2026-10-15/credit-basics-plan/RESULT.md). The scope below remains its historical brief, not a new pending task.

**User outcome:** find a customer quickly, see how much remains unpaid and its age, and receive a clear warning before going beyond the customer's limit.

Scope:

- Define and implement one deterministic FIFO aging calculation from authoritative customer ledger records, as of a business date. Collections reduce the oldest outstanding credit first; no new due-date fields or stored balance/score copies.
- Specify treatment of opening balances, advance collections, corrections/reversals, same-date ordering and malformed/missing history before enabling category labels. Do not mislabel unclassified opening debt as a dated sale.
- Add search to the existing Sale customer picker using stable account IDs and active-customer rules; preserve selection while filtering.
- Show current/projected debt and the advisory credit limit. Deliberately allow continuing after a warning; do not block or silently change the sale amount. Handle no configured limit explicitly.

**Done when:** hand-worked normal/partial-payment/advance/correction and age-boundary examples agree with the calculation; search works with duplicate/long names; warning/cancel/continue causes at most one sale and preserves existing posting, permissions and close rules. Use only affected calculation/facade/UI tests and one representative phone flow.

**Stop:** no new category table, bills, reminders, provider integration, historical ledger rewrite or general retail work.

## Current platform checks and recommended changes to old assumptions

- Google Play restricts SMS permissions and requires eligible uses/declarations; the presence of financial data does not itself establish SEND_SMS eligibility. Android supports SMS composition through an intent. This supports **manual-first as a recommendation**, not a claim that the old automatic-SMS commitment was cancelled. [Google Play policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10208820?hl=en), [Android message intents](https://developer.android.com/guide/components/intents-common.html).
- WorkManager execution can be delayed by system optimizations/constraints. Plan eventual daily detection and rechecking when appropriate, not an exact clock-time sending promise. [Android periodic-work reference](https://developer.android.com/reference/androidx/work/PeriodicWorkRequest).
- The original blueprint's 'zero schema change' applies to ledger-based aging, not the entire feature: its own flags/reminder history require persisted metadata and compatibility work. Current schema must drive those designs.
- No new arbitrary age thresholds, automatic restrictions, provider costs, statutory reporting claims or manual-message delivery receipts have been assumed.
