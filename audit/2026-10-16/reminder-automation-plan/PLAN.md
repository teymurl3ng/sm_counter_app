# Phase 6: finite reminder-automation plan

16 October 2026. Baseline `4fc8457`. [Research](RESEARCH.md) precedes this plan; [UI placement](UI_PLAN.md) is part of it. This is phase 6/L4 of the existing six-phase plan, not a new set of release phases. Initial planning baseline; subsequent A/B progress is recorded below. Defaults below are proposals for review, not business facts or vendor-mandated standards.

## Outcome

The owner gets a quiet prompt and one clear customer worklist when statements or overdue follow-ups need attention. Staff review the latest recorded facts, prepare the existing statement/message and deliberately finish or postpone the task. Two phones see shared review state. Customer sending remains manual unless the separate automatic-SMS route is explicitly approved and implemented.

## Rules chosen for this shop

1. **Keep three concepts separate:** a sale's agreed deadline, the customer's statement-review schedule, and when the owner's phone may notify. Changing any reminder setting never changes a sale agreement, balance, category or Owner flag.
2. **Statement review:** proposed default Sunday, around 10:00 in the shop timezone. Positive recorded balances are eligible for an informational statement review, including balances still within terms. Per-customer options are Use shop schedule, Weekly weekday, Monthly day/last day, Custom every 1–365 days with a first date, or Paused. Monthly day 29–31 clamps to the last valid day without losing the intended day in later months. Payment terms do not silently select a billing schedule; the settings explain the distinction.
3. **Overdue review:** use existing agreed-sale/FIFO calculations. An unpaid amount first becomes overdue under the existing rule after its due date. Create an internal review on that condition; proposed repeat is seven days after an explicit review, adjustable at shop level to 1–30 days. A customer's next statement review may happen sooner. Unknown/partial records go to Check records and never acquire invented late dates. Owner Bad flags remain internal review signals, not a new automatic collection escalation.
4. **One row per customer:** merge current statement/overdue/records reasons. Settled/advance customers have no payment request; already pending items are removed or resolved on refresh. Missing phone does not hide debt or block statement review; show Add number and retain PDF/image access.
5. **Controls:** Owner enables automation and changes global policy/primary phone. Existing full-financial leadership roles may review/prepare through their existing access, subject to the actual server membership role. No Cashier expansion. Pause excludes a customer from the automated worklist until resumed; explicit manual account review remains available. Snooze means Until this date; it does not forgive debt. Mark reviewed schedules the next applicable review and never means Sent or Paid.
6. **Changed facts:** reevaluate on successful sync, on entering the list and before handoff. Payment, corrected debt, archived/inactive customer, changed terms or phone, and schedule revisions may invalidate an item. Re-read the current snapshot and require review again; do not send a frozen queued amount.

## Scheduling and quiet behavior

- Default OFF. On enable, choose one primary notification phone; this is not silently every installation. Proposed preferred time 10:00, quiet hours 20:00–09:00, in the shop timezone. These are adjustable preferences. The worker checks eligibility periodically (proposed six hours) and when the app resumes/sync finishes; WorkManager can run late. No exact-time delivery promise, exact-alarm permission, persistent foreground service or battery-exemption demand.
- At most one generic owner-notification attempt per shop/business day. Merge reasons into that digest; later changes update the in-app attention instead of buzzing per customer. No alert for empty/snoozed/paused work. A swipe-away or notification tap does not mark customer work reviewed.
- Missed runs evaluate current eligible work, not every missed historical notification. Persist pending attention until reviewed, snoozed, paused or no longer eligible. Quiet hours defer the alert; they do not hide the list.
- Denied app/channel permission keeps the list and badge working. Request permission only from the explicit owner enable/settings action. Use a distinct reminder channel, generic lock-screen wording and no names/balances. Deep links pass through normal authentication and full-financial checks.
- The primary phone must validate its current server assignment before reserving a digest attempt. No automatic offline takeover by the second phone. Owner may transfer primary responsibility explicitly while connected. A restored installation must revalidate/reselect; a backup cannot silently become a second primary. If a process dies after reserving an alert but before posting it, prefer a missed alert with persistent in-app work over duplicate repeated alerts. Do not claim exactly-once notification display.
- Background calculation uses prior Owner activation plus current authenticated server assignment, not a manufactured local sign-in session or stored Owner password. Cashier-only/revoked server access stops coordination; opening customer facts still needs the current ordinary full-financial session. An untrusted/unanchored clock must be refreshed before scheduled claims; show the existing time/sync explanation instead of advancing review cycles from a suspicious device date.

## Two-phone design and honest limits

**Selected architecture:** Android computes current eligibility using the existing financial readers; a small shared server metadata contract coordinates policy, customer preferences, review acknowledgment, snooze and claims. A local Room cache supports display/recovery. The financial ledger remains authoritative and is not copied into a second stored balance or rewritten. No microservice, server credit engine, push provider or new identity platform is required for owner reminders.

Proposed metadata boundaries: shop policy/primary-installation revision; per-customer schedule/pause/snooze/review state; append-only minimal actions; and active per-customer claims/daily notification reservation. Exact tables/RPC names and migrations are pinned in piece B before edits. Use the inspected credit-metadata pattern: separate versioned capability, authenticated shop membership and actual server-role checks, private/RLS-protected data, explicit grants and atomic mutations. Never trust user-editable auth metadata or put a server/service key in Android. Contract/role changes require online accepted state; no last-writer-wins offline primary election.

All coordinated in-app reminder launches use the same customer-level gate, including the existing direct manual reminder entry when coordinated mode is enabled. Standalone/manual use before enabling this mode remains as phase 5. In coordinated mode, offline display/draft preparation remains available, but the external reminder launch waits for connection and a claim; explain this tradeoff before enable. Ordinary offline sales, collections and app access are unchanged.

Claims are scoped to shop + customer, with a stable cycle/reason identity for review state. Opening the row alone does not acquire a long lock. Preparation may use a short renewable lease (proposed five minutes). Immediately before external handoff, atomically record Handoff started with an attempt ID. A second phone cannot start another handoff for that customer while this is unresolved. A preparation lease can expire safely; Handoff started must not automatically expire into another send attempt. Crash/unknown return becomes Needs review. The owner explicitly marks reviewed, reports no handoff, or acknowledges uncertainty before retrying. A lock alone cannot prove external cancellation or delivery.

Cycle IDs use the accepted schedule/occurrence, not a fresh UUID each worker run. Command IDs make retries idempotent; one shared action advances next review, and repeated runs/restarts cannot create duplicate rows for the same customer/cycle. The UI merges multiple eligible reasons. Local phase 5 history is not proof of a shared acknowledgment and is not imported as Sent/Reviewed automatically.

Before launch: push/pull through the existing sync gate, reject held/pending required work, load a consistent local snapshot, and compare a server change watermark at the atomic handoff gate. If the head changed, refresh/review again. This catches recorded concurrent payments; it cannot discover a payment still sitting on another offline phone or outside the app. Display freshness honestly. Coordinated claims prevent concurrent app-managed handoffs, not contact from an old app, another messaging app, or a person outside this workflow. Both pilot phones must be updated before enabling coordinated mode; compatibility warnings cannot turn an old client into a compliant sender.

Restore uses cached metadata only as a cache. Fetch current server revisions before scheduling/handoff, reconcile pending command IDs, do not replay notifications/customer contact from backup, and never replace newer accepted primary/pause/review state with older backup values. A shop without enrolled sync can keep manual phase 5 functionality; coordinated automation stays unavailable with an explanation.

## Automatic SMS: recorded decision, not a silent omission

### Devices without a SIM

**Confirmed shop setup:** the shop SIM is in a separate dumb phone; the operator tablet cannot accept that SIM. The dumb phone cannot be assumed to run this app, linked messaging software or an SMS gateway. Having the shop SIM does not itself connect tablet drafts to cellular SMS. A06/S24 QA availability is not evidence that either is a permanent shop sender.

The initial plan's provider route already avoids dependence on the operator device's SIM, but it did not explicitly cover Wi-Fi-only tablets in the manual fallback. This supplement closes that gap without adding another build piece or a tablet layout redesign. A tablet can review accounts, prepare statements/drafts and receive ordinary app notifications without telephony. It may be the primary owner-notification device; that assignment does not make it an SMS-sending device.

- **WhatsApp:** a configured linked WhatsApp tablet app can use its internet connection without a SIM in the tablet. Check the actual app handoff and handle an unlinked/unavailable app honestly; installation alone is not proof of setup, message acceptance or delivery. No browser fallback is called a successful WhatsApp composer.
- **SMS:** the presence of an SMS composer does not prove that this device can send cellular SMS. Some messaging apps may relay through a linked phone; allow their deliberate composer path and describe it as a handoff, not a verified cellular route. Do not blanket-disable composition solely because local telephony is absent, and do not request broad phone/SIM access just to guess availability. A future direct-SIM sender would need telephony capability, a usable explicitly selected subscription, permission and carrier checks; the manual app does not silently become that sender.
- **No working composer:** retain the reviewed draft with a useful explanation and the existing PDF/image Android share action. If an app-capable authorized smartphone is actually available, continuing this customer's review there can use the same shared follow-up state, with refreshed facts and confirmed recipient. The confirmed dumb phone cannot provide this in-app continuation: its manual SMS fallback requires the operator to compose/send the reviewed wording on the phone itself, and the app cannot observe that sending. Do not imply an automatic tablet-to-phone SMS relay, transfer the private one-draft override silently, or mark the message sent. Missing-handler wording must not insist on SMS as the only fallback when it may also be unavailable.
- **Unattended SMS:** a selected server/provider route can dispatch independently of tablet SIM hardware. Initiating/reviewing it needs the internet/server contract; it does not promise offline sending or a message that was never accepted by the server. If the dedicated-SIM alternative is chosen, name an app-capable separate phone explicitly; the existing dumb phone is not an implemented gateway and cannot be assumed to support one. The tablet is only an operator client.

Include no-telephony/no-composer and linked-app handoff fixtures inside A/B's existing affected acceptance. A physical tablet/large-screen layout remains a separate retained presentation scope; capability/fallback correctness must not wait for that redesign. No silent channel switching, new gateway service, automatic personal-phone pairing or additional provider purchase belongs to this correction.

Piece C records a concrete go/no-go recommendation using current distribution (private signed APK pilot), estimated customer/message volume, sender availability, customer consent/preferences, opt-out mechanism, GSM/Unicode segment cost, daytime schedule, spend/rate caps and provider retry/status contract. No volume, legal eligibility or price is invented. Jazz and Twilio are research leads, not an approved integration. WhatsApp Business API, payment links and bulk campaigns remain separate retained work.

**Owner clarification during A:** shop activity fluctuates; no fixed customer-count or permanent volume estimate is available or required. C must compare provider unit/segment charges and representative ranges, then estimate each actual run from current eligible recipients, encoding and segment count. Show the estimate before activation/run and use owner-adjustable daily/monthly spend/message ceilings with clear remaining allowance. A ceiling is a deliberate control, not an invented forecast or a hardcoded customer limit. If price/quota is unknown, do not automatically incur unbounded cost. Final limits and provider terms are selected at configuration time; this does not block A/B.

If a provider is selected later, credentials stay server-side. Use one durable outbox and stable operation key per customer/cycle/channel, fresh data/recipient checks and a central dispatcher. Distinguish Queued, Provider accepted, Provider-reported delivered, Failed and Unknown; never Read or Paid. Ambiguous acceptance must not trigger blind resend. Automatic sending stays off until the actual provider contract, writer-freshness policy, consent, cost controls and narrowly scoped synthetic acceptance are approved. A server cannot know unuploaded offline payments either; that limitation must be part of the enable decision. This plan authorizes no provider account, purchase or customer contact.

## Exactly three bounded pieces: two builds and one decision

**A — Rules and planned screens.** Build a pure, deterministic eligibility/cycle calculator plus native list/settings/customer-preference/review presentation using existing readers and tokens. Use injected settings/fake scheduling in QA; keep unfinished controls out of the ordinary app. Pin affected tests before coding. Finish: Sunday versus overdue reasons, weekly/monthly/custom dates, payment/correction/partial evidence and normal/dark/large-text layouts agree with the examples below. One phone is sufficient.

**B — Shared state and quiet scheduling.** Implement the small versioned coordination contract, Room migration/cache, authenticated transport, restore behavior, WorkManager/notifier, in-app attention and shared handoff gate. DEV/synthetic verification first, then a separately reviewable exact PROD migration and signed delivery through the existing handover. Finish: two phones cannot acquire concurrent coordinated handoff, pending/uncertain states survive retry/restore, settled work disappears, denied notifications keep the list, primary transfer and quiet/missed runs follow the frozen rules. Two phones are required only for the final concurrency/recovery checks.

**C — Automatic-SMS decision and acceptance record.** Compare the two realistic unattended routes against the actual pilot's costs/operation; record the recommended route and activation prerequisites plainly. This is a decision deliverable, not a claim that automatic customer sending was implemented. If requested, its provider/SIM integration receives its own exact additions/tests within the retained sending commitment before execution. Owner reminder automation can be accepted while customer sending remains explicitly inactive; do not mark that sending feature delivered.

## Fixed acceptance examples / stop line

- Sale 16 October + seven days: due 23 October, first overdue review 24 October. Sunday 18 October may request a statement review, but cannot call that sale late. Existing mixed-sale terms stay attached to each sale.
- 31 January + one calendar month follows existing term calculation (28 February 2027); a statement scheduled on the 31st separately clamps in February and returns to the 31st in March. A custom interval is anchored to its first date, not worker start time.
- Full payment/advance, correction, deactivation, pause or snooze before action invalidates the payment request. Partial/undated debt never produces a fabricated deadline. One customer with three sales and two reasons gets one row, not six messages.
- Repeated worker/foreground triggers, midnight/shop-timezone boundaries, permission denial, notification swipe, three missed weeks, restart after reserving a digest, primary change, restore and two simultaneous phones have literal expected outcomes. No catch-up spam or offline second-primary election.
- Race after claim/before handoff rechecks the server watermark and current facts. Lost reservation response and crash after Handoff started return one durable uncertain attempt, not a silently released resend. User-reported reviewed/unsent is labeled as such.
- Customer/schedule changes and snooze leave header/footer/action geometry stable; names, full amounts and actions remain readable at 200% through vertical scrolling. A notification cannot bypass sign-in/role checks or expose customer debt on a lock screen.
- Pin only calculator, affected reader/UI, coordination/RPC/transport, migration/backup and worker tests as each piece begins. No whole-app suite, real customer receiver, paid provider or unrelated audit. A new finding goes to LATER, not a fourth build piece.
