# Research and adaptation

Primary sources inspected on 2026-10-11. The following are design guidance and accessibility benchmarks, not proof that a particular hue will be liked by this shop's users.

1. [Android mobile themes](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/mobile/guides/styles/themes): night surfaces use low tonal values; consider type settings and check foreground/background contrast. **Adaptation:** change the large mint header to a dark forest surface. Keep its geometry and content slots stable. Do not globally darken primary, which also controls selected items and actions.
2. [Android mobile color](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/mobile/guides/styles/color): use semantic tokens and a limited, consistent palette; excessive decorative colors overwhelm; color must not be the only action cue. **Adaptation:** distinct roles for header, identity, supporting text, numeric emphasis and actions. Preserve actual text labels for status. Mint continues to identify actions/selection rather than every ordinary value.
3. [Material 3 theming codelab](https://developer.android.com/codelabs/m3-design-theming): hierarchy uses font size, weight, line height and spacing; text roles are named by purpose. **Adaptation:** retain Manrope, use medium labels/subtitles and semibold values, and separate scan positions for names, references and money. Avoid switching the whole app to heavier body typography.
4. [IBM Carbon data-table guidance](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/core/components/data-table/guidelines): consistent component sizing/alignment and sufficient room help dense data avoid cramped presentation. **Adaptation:** repeated mobile rows use the same anatomy and predictable metric position; grow or stack when content requires it. Carbon's desktop pixel heights, hover behavior and table truncation are not copied into portrait touch lists.
5. [W3C contrast minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html): normal text needs 4.5:1; large text has a 3:1 exception. **Adaptation:** use >=4.5:1 for every enabled text role in this plan, even headings, to avoid relying on the exception. Measure the actual composited background, including selection and chips.
6. [W3C use of color](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html): supply a visible alternative when color conveys meaning. **Adaptation:** names, references and metrics also differ by weight/position; status keeps wording and corrections keep their existing markers. The list remains understandable in grayscale.

These sources support role-based hierarchy and darker night surfaces. They do **not** prescribe deep forest #20372D, warm amount #E9D6A7, or our exact font weights. Those are proposed app-specific choices, measured in CONTRAST.json and still subject to installed-app visual review. Contrast passing demonstrates legibility potential, not aesthetic approval or complete accessibility certification. Shopify Polaris typography could not be fetched in this pass and is not used as evidence.
