# Primary-source design research

Checked 11 October 2026. These sources establish useful patterns; they do not establish that our exact styling has been user-tested or mandates a capsule on every setting.

|Source|Verified guidance/example|Adaptation to this app|
|---|---|---|
|[Android: organize settings](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/components/settings/organize-your-settings)|Related settings can be grouped under category titles; large hierarchies can use subscreens.|Group by shop identity, rates, opening state and categories. Reuse existing edit screens. The guidance is for Views, but the information-organisation principle transfers to Compose; no Preference-library migration needed.|
|[Android: layout basics](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/mobile/guides/layout-and-content/layout-basics)|Containment groups related content/actions; alignment and consistent spacing support readability.|Use shared section insets and fact alignment, retain safe areas and stable chrome. Keep this product's agreed portrait scope even though general Android guidance considers other orientations.|
|[Android Compose: Card](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/components/card)|Cards represent coherent content, distinct from whole-screen structure; filled/outlined choices are supported.|A related section gets one warm outlined surface. Do not turn each field, caption or paragraph into a card.|
|[Android Compose: Divider](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/components/divider)|Thin separators distinguish items within lists/containers.|Optional inset separators within a grouped fact block. Avoid separators outside rows that already have a border.|
|[Android Compose: accessibility defaults](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/accessibility/api-defaults)|Interactive targets should be at least 48dp; selection behavior can live on the row; semantics reflect control purpose.|Retain full-row actions and clear control roles; read-only facts must not pretend to be navigation buttons.|
|[Square: account and business information](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/3861-edit-your-account-and-business-settings)|Documents separate personal-information/security and business-information areas; app route is More → Settings → Account → Business information, with Basic Information.|Separate shop configuration from personal security. Keep business details under a named group. This verifies structure and routes, not current card appearance.|
|[Shopify POS: app settings](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/getting-started/app-settings)|Documents task-specific settings routes such as Settings → Dark/light mode and Settings → Screen lock.|Use recognizable task names and related settings groups. Our milk-retail rates/opening data need their own business-specific grouping. No inference that Shopify uses our proposed shape or spacing.|

Our recommendation: retain the existing native palette, Manrope typography and 12/16dp shape system. Add restrained section grouping where it is missing. Exact row dimensions and responsive rules below are our design decisions, to be checked on A06/S24; they are not industry-mandated measurements beyond the touch-target requirement.
