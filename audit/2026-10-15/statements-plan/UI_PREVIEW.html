PHASE 5 · PLANNING STUDY · SYNTHETIC DATA

A place for statements,
without a wall of buttons.

One entry on customer detail opens a focused statement task. Preparing a message gets its own review pane. The existing Customers header and role navigation stay in place.

  1. Customer detail: one balance/status summary, one new task entry, quiet disclosures for other information.
  2. Statement: period, document preview, one Share action. PDF/images are a small follow-up choice.
  3. Reminder: recipient, channel, message, then a clearly labelled external handoff.

Click the phone controls. Nothing is exported or sent. The paper remains light in night mode. All names, numbers and amounts here are fictional.

Uses existing native palette, typography and surface geometry. Chrome proportions and browser text rendering are illustrative; Android inset, keyboard, accessibility and 200% font acceptance remain implementation checks.
